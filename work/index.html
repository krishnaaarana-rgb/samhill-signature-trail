Featured Project

Coronet,
Queenstown.

A signature alpine trail network, designed and built end-to-end across one of the most demanding landscapes in the southern hemisphere.

Location Queenstown, NZ
Terrain Alpine, exposed schist
Scope Design + Build
Delivery Managed end-to-end

Built across the steep, exposed terrain above Lake Wakatipu, this project demanded a build that could honour the alpine landscape and still deliver the flow our team puts our name to. The result is a network of machine shaped and compacted berms, progressive jumps, and natural-feature lines cut directly into the contours of the mountainside.

Our crew managed the full build on site, from first earthworks through to final ride-off. Every line was shaped by the team that designed it, and every feature was tested by the people who built it.

Build Highlights
  • Machine-shaped, compacted berms
  • Progressive jump line
  • Natural-feature lines cut to contour
  • Schist-aware drainage & surfacing
  • Build crew on site for full duration
A signature berm carved into the mountainside, with the township and lake below.
Our in-house build crew on site, shaping lines from the saddle of the ridge.
Exhilarating and technical section of trail designed for a tough piece of terrain where the machine skill required was second to none.
