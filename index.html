About Sam Hill

A Legacy Built on the Trail.
A Business Built to Deliver.

Sam Hill is one of the most decorated mountain bikers in history, an 8x World Champion with over 20 years competing at the highest level across 30+ countries. That career gave Sam something no engineer or landscaper has: an instinct for how trails actually ride.

Sam Hill Signature Trail is the full package, covering consultancy, design, and construction under one roof. Our in-house construction capability, backed by deep expertise in sustainable trail building and land management, means every project is delivered to the highest professional standard, from first concept to final ride-off.
